BIOGRAPHY

THE SOUND OF MOONWALKER

Hailing from Amsterdam, Moonwalker is a techno producer crafting immersive journeys through sound. Blending techno with the raw energy of hard techno and other influences, his music is designed to move both the body and the mind. Productions explore the balance between emotion and intensity, seamlessly shifting from melodic moments to harder, relentless passages. Every track tells a story.
